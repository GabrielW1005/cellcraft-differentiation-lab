    How to read the model

    Each mission begins with a different kind of precursor. The purple signal and two gene-activity markers stand for parts of a real developmental process. A developing cell responds to signals, and patterns of gene activity help it build structures that suit its job.

    Swimming, health, dashes and drifting obstacles are game mechanics. Real cells develop over time in their tissues. Most cells in one organism contain the same DNA and use different parts of it. In a real muscle, developing cells fuse to form a skeletal muscle fiber.
